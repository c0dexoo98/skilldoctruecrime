# Production workflow

1. **Intake:** capture language, Shorts/long-form, duration, platform, audience, themes, case status, narration plan and picture-source rule (stills-only / mixed / supplied footage). Record motion energy and sound-design density if relevant.
2. **Topic slate:** offer five to eight varied topics. For each, state the hook, short summary, format fit, sourceability and cautious audience-interest rationale. Ask the creator to choose.
3. **Research:** build a claim ledger from primary records and reputable corroborating sources. Mark disputed details and research date.
4. **Script approval:** write timed narration with hook, context, evidence-led development and grounded ending. Attach source ledger, visual beat map, on-screen text and asset provenance. Ask for approval.
5. **Audio:** accept supplied narration or, only after approval, synthesize the approved text using an available supported voice. Measure actual audio duration before locking picture; preserve supplied originals.
6. **Picture:** map each narration beat to a sourced still, original diagram, clearly labeled illustration or approved footage, respecting the selected source mode. For stills-only, forbid every video clip as an input. Keep generated images distinct from evidence.
7. **Motion and edit:** treat each still as a scene. Use motivated push/pull, parallax, animated crops, match cuts, paper wipes and concise snap/whip transitions. Draw red underlines, circles, arrows and clue connections as visible vector-stroke reveals; ensure the line explains the narrative and does not imply unsupported facts.
8. **Sound:** keep narration intelligible; duck a restrained score; sync paper, pencil, pin/click, mechanical switch and whoosh Foley only to meaningful visible actions. Avoid sound cues that imply undocumented events.
9. **QA:** verify runtime, aspect ratio, frame rate, codecs, audio sync and full-file decoding. Inspect opening, stable reading holds, key transitions, captions and end card. Verify provenance/reconstruction labels and, for stills-only, confirm every picture source is a still or graphic.
10. **Delivery:** provide MP4, approved script, beat map/storyboard, source/asset ledger, reusable project/edit sources and relevant license/attribution notes.

## Gate conditions

Do not research or script a selected case before the creator has selected a topic. Do not render the final piece before script approval. Do not publish factual claims without traceable sources. Do not describe a short AI clip as a complete long-form video. If the creator says “stills only,” never generate, download or use video footage.
